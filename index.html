Our Story

We Believe in the Power of Pure Moments

Black & White Creative was born from a simple belief — that the most honest emotions are captured when you strip away distraction. We blend cinematic videography with fine-art photography to create wedding memories that endure across generations.

Based in India, we travel wherever love takes us. Each wedding is approached as a unique narrative — your story, told with intention and soul.
